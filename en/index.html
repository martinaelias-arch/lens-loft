Families

Your family. Exactly as it is right now.

Kids grow up fast. And no, they don't need to sit still and smile obediently for ten minutes. We can shoot at home, outdoors, on a trip, or anywhere you feel comfortable. I want you to look back in a few years and recognize not just how you looked, but what it felt like.

Family photography

You don't need to know how to pose. Seriously.

Before the shoot

First we talk about what you need photographed and what the photos will be used for. Based on that, we pick a location, figure out what we'll shoot, and agree on everything important.

During the shoot

I won't put you in front of a wall and say "now smile." I'll guide you a little, let things flow a little, and above all keep an eye on the light, composition and the moment it all comes together.

After the shoot

You'll get carefully selected and edited photos in an online gallery.

Why Light & Loft?

Because I don't enjoy taking photos just to have photos. I want them to make sense.

  • For brand shoots, I think about where you'll actually use the photos.
  • For interiors, about what makes the space interesting.
  • For events, about what people will take away from it.
  • For weddings, about what you'll want to see in ten years.
  • And for families, about what you'll miss the most one day.

And then, of course, there's the light. I love that a lot.

I'm Martina.

I've been photographing for over 10 years, and what I still love most is that every shoot is a little different.

One day I'm with a businesswoman who needs new photos for her brand. Another day I'm photographing an interior, working out every piece of light. Then I spend a whole day at a wedding, or I'm running around outside after a family with kids.

And that variety is exactly what I love. I don't have one type of person I photograph. I like people who create something, work on themselves, or simply want to hold on to a piece of their life, and who see the value in that.
