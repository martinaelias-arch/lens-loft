Ask about your wedding

Your wedding, your rules.

Maybe this is your first wedding. Maybe your second.

Maybe you want a big celebration with everyone you love. Maybe just a small ceremony and dinner with your closest people.

And maybe you already know you don't want to do things just because that's "how it's done."

That's completely fine.

What matters most to me is how you want to experience your day.

I don't want you posing all day.

I want you to enjoy it.

Of course I'll photograph the two of you together. We'll photograph family, friends, the rings, the dress, the bouquet, and all the important things. But for most of the day, I'll leave you be.

I'll move around you, waiting for the moments that happen on their own. Laughter. Nerves. Tears. A hug. A glance across the whole table. A friend who went a little overboard with the dancing. A grandmother laughing somewhere in the corner.

Because these are exactly the things I think you'll want to remember years from now.

I photograph weddings that feel like you.

You don't need perfectly color-coordinated decorations.

You don't need a wedding coordinator, a minute-by-minute schedule, or thirty traditions.

You can have everything. Or almost nothing. What matters is that it's yours.

And I'll adapt to whatever happens throughout the day.

How it works

01

You write to me

Tell me when and where you're getting married and what kind of wedding you're planning. If you have an idea about the photos, feel free to share it right away. If not, that's totally fine.

02

We meet

Before the wedding we'll have a meeting or video call. I want to get to know you, discuss your day, and find out what matters to you. We'll also go through the schedule, venue, and all the practical details.

03

I show up and shoot

On the wedding day, I'll be wherever something is happening. I'll guide you a little when we need to photograph something together, but otherwise I'll try to stay as unobtrusive as possible. You enjoy the wedding, I'll keep an eye on everything around you.

04

You choose the photos

After the wedding you'll get a preview gallery. You choose which photos you want finally edited. I want you to have photos that you love above all.

05

You get the finished photos

I'll edit your selected photos and you'll receive them in an online gallery in high resolution.

What do I photograph?

Preparations

The dress, the suit, the bouquet, the rings, the nerves and the first drink.

Ceremony

The entrance, the vows, the rings, the kiss and all the reactions around you.

Group photos

Family, friends, and of course the two of you. Without an hour-long session of every possible combination.

Portraits of the two of you

We'll steal a moment during the day just for the two of you. Don't worry · I won't ask for twenty different poses.

Reception and celebration

Food, toasts, the cake, laughter, conversations, dancing, and everything else that happens during the evening.

Details and moments in between

Because these are often the most beautiful ones.

How much time will we spend together?

That depends on the wedding you're planning. Some couples want a photographer from the morning preparations all the way through the evening party. Others just need the ceremony, portraits, and the start of the reception captured.

You don't need to book a full day if you don't need one.

Together we'll choose the scope that makes sense for your specific wedding.

What if you're not photogenic?

I've photographed plenty of couples like that. And just like with any other shoot, you don't need to know how to pose.

When needed, I'll tell you where to stand and what to do. But most of the time, I'll just let you be together.

Because the loveliest photos often happen the moment you forget there's a photographer behind you.

The wedding is one day.
The photos will stay.

You might not remember exactly what everyone said. You might forget how the band played during the first dance. But in ten, twenty or thirty years, you'll want to see how you laughed back then. Who was there. What your children looked like. How your parents looked at you. And that's exactly why, in my opinion, a wedding photographer matters.
