I want a family shoot

Kids grow up.

Something is always changing, and it feels like just yesterday they were tiny.

And then suddenly you realize you have an almost-teenager at home.

That's why I think it makes sense to stop time once in a while and get photographed just as you are together right now.

Not perfectly. Just the way it is.

From family photoshoots

Outdoors and at home, from newborns to families with teenagers. Click a photo to enlarge.

You don't need to know how to pose.

And your kids definitely don't either.

I don't expect them to sit still next to each other for half an hour, smiling at the lens. Honestly, I wouldn't even want that.

I'd much rather let you do something together, talk, run around, goof off, or just lounge around. I'll photograph in the meantime. And I'll guide you a little when needed.

Where, what and how

Where can we shoot?

We can shoot at your home.

Or we can head outside, into nature, into the city, to a favorite spot, on a trip, or somewhere your family likes to go.

The more the setting is truly yours, the more natural the photos usually turn out.

What will we do during the shoot?

We can just go for a walk. You can play with the kids. You can read a book or all pile onto the couch together.

An outdoor shoot can be a walk, a trip, a forest, a meadow, the city, or a place you love.

I don't want to turn you into a model family. I want to photograph your family.

What if the kids don't cooperate?

Then they won't. It happens. :)

Sometimes kids run off in the opposite direction from where we need them. Sometimes they just don't feel like it. Sometimes they argue at the exact moment we wanted a lovely group photo.

That's totally fine. We won't build the shoot around everyone cooperating the whole time. Often, the best photos happen exactly when we stop pushing for something.

Don't have small kids? No problem.

Family photography isn't just for families with toddlers. I also photograph families with older kids and teenagers.

And actually, that's exactly when a shoot together can be a great excuse to go somewhere together for a while.

You don't need to line anyone up. We can go on a trip, walk around the city, grab a coffee or something tasty, and I'll photograph you along the way.

Because even if your kids seem to stay the same to you, in a few years you'll be amazed at how quickly they changed.

When is the best time?

Honestly? Any time you feel like having photos together.

You don't need to wait for Christmas, a birthday, or some anniversary.

We can shoot in spring, when everything is blooming. By the water in summer. In a colorful forest in autumn. Or even at home under a blanket in winter.

Every season has something to offer.

How the shoot works

01

We talk it through

You tell me how many of you there are, how old the kids are, and what you have in mind. We'll pick a location and set a date based on that.

02

We choose a location

At home, outdoors, in the city, in nature, or somewhere you like to go. I'll help you pick based on the kind of photos you want.

03

We shoot

No complicated poses. We'll do something together, talk, and enjoy the time together. I'll be looking for good light, moments, and all the little things that belong to you.

04

You choose the photos

After the shoot you'll get a preview gallery. You choose which photos you want finally edited.

05

You get the finished photos

I'll edit your selected photos and send them to you in an online gallery.

What should you wear?

You don't all need to dress the same.

Ideally, you should feel comfortable in whatever you're wearing.

If you'd like, we can discuss colors and outfits before the shoot so everything works well together with the location.

Photos that won't stay
just on your phone.

You'll receive your selected photos in an online gallery and can download them to your computer or phone. And if you have them printed, they might become even more valuable one day. Because some photos, I believe, belong on a wall or in a photo book, not just in a folder somewhere on a computer.
