What will we photograph together?

Every brand is different, so the shoot doesn't have to look the same every time. We can photograph, for example:

Portraits

Natural photos of you that you can use on your website, social media or in the press.

At work

What you actually do. Meetings, consultations, creating, working with clients, or packing your products.

Details

Handiwork, your workspace, tools, products, materials, and all the small things that shape your brand.

Environment

Your office, studio, premises, a café, nature, or any other place that belongs to you.

Brand life

Because your business isn't just about how you look in a portrait.

First we'll talk about what you need.

Before the shoot, we'll go through where you'll be using the photos and what's missing from your visual content today.

We'll take a look at your website, social media, or other materials, if you have them.

Based on that, we'll work out what to photograph, where to shoot, what to wear, and how to put the whole day together.

I don't want to give you
fifty similar portraits.

I want you to walk away with a set of different photos that will last you a good while.

What if you can't pose?

That's totally fine, most people don't know what to do in front of a camera. I'll tell you where to stand and what to do, and the less you think about being photographed, the better the photos usually turn out.

How the shoot works

01

We talk it through

You write to me about what you need and what you have in mind. We discuss your business, your idea for the shoot, and where you want to use the photos.

02

We plan the shoot

Together we choose the location, outfits, props, and the individual scenes we want to capture.

03

We shoot

I grab my camera and you can focus on what you normally do. I'll guide you a little, let things flow a little, and above all keep an eye on light, composition and making the photos feel natural.

04

I select and edit

After the shoot, I go through all the photos, pick the best ones, and edit them so they work together as a set.

05

You get a gallery

I'll send you the finished photos in an online gallery, from where you can easily download them.

What will you get?

Not just new profile pictures. You'll get a whole set of photos you can use as needed.

Portraits. Photos of you at work. Details. Environment. Horizontal and vertical photos.

So you'll have something to put on your website, Instagram, in your newsletter, or in a new presentation.

And most importantly, so you won't be stuck a month from now thinking:
"Ugh, I have nothing to post on Instagram again."

Who is brand photography for?

For entrepreneurs who want to show their work a bit more personally. For those who:

  • Are launching a new website or rebranding
  • Are just starting out and need their first proper photos
  • Have moved their business forward and their old photos no longer fit
  • Want more visibility on social media
  • Need photos for a new product or service
  • Or simply realized it's finally time for proper photos

You don't need to be an influencer.

You don't need to be an extrovert. You don't need to be an influencer. And you don't need a perfectly worked-out brand.

You just need to want your photos to finally match who you are and what you do.

We shoot wherever it makes sense

We don't need to be in a studio. We can shoot at your office, at home, at your premises, in the city, in nature, in a café, or anywhere that suits your brand.
